FIELD GUIDE: HOW TO PLAY & SPRITES

CONTROLS & ACTIONS

  • Move: WASD, arrow keys, or the direction buttons. Face a tree, rock, or ore vein, then press Space to mine it. It takes three strikes; trees give wood, rocks give stone, and glowing ore gives stone and a crystal.
  • Interact: Press E near a person or object. Interact with the Study Shrine to answer an AP question, the Field Guide for a hint, the workbench to open crafting, the campfire to restore 15 HP, or a rabbit to get a berry.
  • Practice: Choose your course above the game. A correct shrine answer earns a knowledge crystal, a berry, and 8 XP. The shrine needs 10 seconds to prepare another question.
  • Craft: Stand within 5 tiles of the workbench, open CRAFT, and choose a recipe. Recipes show their material cost. Two berries can be crafted into a meal that restores 25 HP.
  • Build: Select planks, walls, torches, or a campfire in the hotbar, face an open tile, and press B. Planks make floor tiles; the other items place objects.
  • Survive: Select berries and press H to eat one and restore 12 HP. Slimes chase and damage you; press K or ATTACK when one is close. Two hits defeat a slime for gel, with a chance of an extra berry. At 0 HP, respawn at camp.
  • Hotbar: Press 1–8 or tap a slot to select an item. Your world and inventory save in this browser.

SPRITE & WORLD LEGEND

  • Adventurer: Your profile character. Its outfit and class appear in the wilds; it turns to face the direction you last moved.
  • Rabbit: Friendly wildlife. Interact nearby to collect a berry; it hops around the meadow.
  • Slime: Hostile green creature. It follows nearby players and takes two hits to defeat.
  • Tree: Mine for wood. Gray rock: Mine for stone. Glowing teal ore: Mine for stone and a crystal.
  • Study Shrine: Glowing teal-and-gold monument for AP practice. Correct answers reward crystals and berries.
  • Field Guide: Purple-robed helper with a gold marker; interact for a gameplay hint.
  • Workbench: Wooden table near camp; use it to craft. Cabin: Your home base. Campfire: Interact to recover HP.
  • Terrain: Green is meadow, brown is dirt, blue is water (blocked), golden sand is shore, and a tiled brown floor is player-built.
